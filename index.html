01. About Me

Rooted in curiosity, driven by purpose

I'm Shagun Choudhary, a Medical Biotechnology student in my 5th semester at Amity University, Noida. My journey began with a simple question: what makes a cell decide its fate? That curiosity has since blossomed into a deep passion for understanding life at the molecular level.

From mastering aseptic technique to designing CRISPR guides, every experiment teaches me something new about the beautiful complexity of biological systems. I believe the most meaningful science happens at the intersection of disciplines — where biology meets computation, where chemistry informs medicine, where curiosity meets rigor.

I'm actively seeking research internships where I can contribute to projects in precision oncology, gene therapy, or infectious disease biology. If you're working on something fascinating, let's talk.
